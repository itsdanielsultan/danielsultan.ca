About me · Mississauga, Canada

I like making things

I'm Daniel Sultan. I build websites, 3D models and learning tools, usually starting with a question I want to understand better myself.

How do new buildings fit into a whole city? How do you make financial regulation easier to learn? Can a small piece of software remove an everyday frustration? My projects are ways of working through those questions and sharing something useful.

What I'm building

Ideas that become something you can try.

Future Mississauga began with wanting to see the city's future buildings in context. I taught myself Blender and brought together City data, existing models, development drawings and my own modelling. It has grown into an independent city atlas, with sources and revisions kept alongside the pictures. 905HUB featured the visuals in September 2026.

InvestmentRegulation.ca and IFC Prep came from my own study. Both are free, independent learning resources with explanations, practice and public source code.

I also build smaller tools. Caps Lock is published in the Raycast Store. Other experiments use local models to help organise notes, shape a work outline or choose an outdoor break.

How I work

Build, check, improve.

I like work that can be inspected. A useful project needs a working link, a clear explanation and an honest account of its limits. Better references change a city model; trying a game changes its scoring; a small interface change can make a study session easier to follow.

That approach also comes from working with people. My experience spans financial sales, high-volume customer service, classroom support, tutoring, coaching and camp leadership. These settings have taught me to listen, explain things plainly and follow through.
