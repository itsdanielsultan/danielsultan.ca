# Employer, education and activity images

Added September 6, 2026. The PNGs are stored locally and identify the named employers,
school and training provider. They do not imply sponsorship or endorsement. Brand
artwork remains the property of its respective owner. Images retain their source
colors and proportions. No diploma, certificate, employer logo or award seal was
invented.

## Activity icons (96 × 96 PNG)

`tent-tree`, `book-open`, `users-round`, `heart-pulse`, `award`, `graduation-cap`
and `code-xml` are original Lucide icons, rasterized without changing their paths.
They represent activities or categories, not organization logos or official awards.
