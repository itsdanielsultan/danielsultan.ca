Financial learning tools.

Making technical finance material easier to work through, one explanation and one practice session at a time.

I built InvestmentRegulation.ca and IFC Prep alongside my own finance study, bringing lessons, practice and review into a clear learning flow.

Start with understanding.

The first version of InvestmentRegulation.ca took three weeks. I organised the material to begin with “What is investing?” before moving into products, regulation and professional responsibilities. The syllabus set the scope; the explanations needed a foundation a new learner could follow.

Read the build story

InvestmentRegulation.ca

The CIRE resource brings together 69 lessons across eight chapters and a 100-question bank, alongside flashcards, drills, a glossary and mock practice. I put teaching before checks and kept missed-question review close to the activity.

Progress and bookmarks stay in the learner’s browser. The resource is free, with no account required.

IFC Prep

The separate Investment Funds in Canada companion extends the same approach with original lessons, worked examples, practice questions and written-answer exercises. It includes 54 lessons and 18 chapter workshops, plus a downloadable and printable companion.

Why this work matters to me.

These projects connect my finance study with the work of explaining things clearly. I had to organise technical material, write practice that follows the teaching, and build a tool someone can use from beginning to end.

Both projects publish their code and original study material under the MIT licence, so people can inspect the work and suggest corrections.

Sources and scope.

These are independent study resources. Practice questions are original exercises, not official exam questions. IFC Prep is not affiliated with or endorsed by CSI and does not replace the purchased course.
