Financial learning tools

Two free, independent study tools that put explanations, practice and review in the same place. I built them to give learners a clear route through technical finance material, starting with the ideas behind the terminology.

Open InvestmentRegulation.caOpen IFC Prep

Start with understanding

The first version of InvestmentRegulation.ca took three weeks. I organised the material into a sequence that begins with “What is investing?” before moving into products, regulation and professional responsibilities. The syllabus set the scope; the explanations needed a foundation a new learner could follow.

Read the build story

InvestmentRegulation.ca

The CIRE resource includes 69 lessons across eight chapters and a 100-question bank, alongside flashcards, drills, a glossary and mock practice. I placed teaching before checks and kept missed-question review close to the activity. Progress and bookmarks stay in the learner’s browser, without an account.

IFC Prep

The separate Investment Funds in Canada companion extends that approach with 54 original lessons, 18 chapter workshops, 324 assessment questions and 162 flashcards. Worked examples and written-answer exercises give learners room to explain their reasoning. Downloadable HTML and a printable companion support study away from the live site.

Keep the work inspectable

I use distinct lesson, practice and review modes, with keyboard controls and light and dark themes. Both projects publish their source under the MIT licence, so the implementation and original practice material can be inspected and corrected.

Independent study resources

These tools do not establish licensing, registration or exam readiness. Practice questions are not official exam questions. IFC Prep is not affiliated with or endorsed by CSI and does not replace the purchased course. Rules and exam requirements change; learners should check current official sources.
