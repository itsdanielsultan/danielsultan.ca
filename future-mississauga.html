Future Mississauga

Buildings, transit and development proposals in a city you can move through. I combine municipal data, existing models, public drawings and my own modelling to explore how Mississauga is changing.

Open the atlasBrowse the gallery

Seeing the city together

A development rendering shows one site. I wanted to understand how new buildings and transit fit into the wider city. The atlas brings that context together through neighbourhood views, an interactive explorer and comparisons from the same viewpoint.

Building with the sources

I check footprints, addresses and orientation against available drawings and photographs, then review the model beside roads, terrain and neighbouring buildings. I keep existing context, recorded construction and future proposals distinct. Gallery images and model releases carry their own dates.

1355 Winding Trail

The mapped building was missing much of its visible exterior. References shared by Kleinfeldt Mychajlowycz Architects helped me add the tower, glazed front bays and lower green roofs. These published views use the same viewpoint.

References and estimates

Architectural references shared by Kleinfeldt Mychajlowycz Architects. The City footprint provides the starting scale and shape.

Tower height, roof levels and unseen surfaces remain estimates. The ground is a display base, not a survey.

What the model can tell you

This is independent work, with no official endorsement. Some forms are schematic, hidden details are estimated and source dates vary. A proposal is not proof of approval or construction. Remaining gaps and revisions stay visible in the public review.
