investmentregulation.ca

69Lessons
100Questions
8Chapters
3Weeks

I built this because the official CIRE material tells you what to know, but does not teach it from the beginning.

The site turns the syllabus into 69 plain-language lessons, flashcards, practice rounds and a full mock exam. It starts with “What is investing?” and works up to the material people are expected to know for the exam.

It is free and open source. There is no account, and study progress stays in the person’s own browser.

Jun 2026Physical prototype

Synapse memory-game prototype

Stage: physical prototype · Informal classmate testing

One person lays out a pattern of shapes on a board. The other person looks at it, the board gets covered, and they try to put it back the same way. You score on how close you got, not how fast you were.

I made the board and the tiles by hand. The hard part was working out how many shapes to use, too few and nobody gets it wrong, too many and nobody gets it right.

Jun 2026Completed school project

Electromagnetic physics demonstration

Stage: completed · Working crane, experiment and project website

If you wrap wire around a piece of metal and run electricity through it, the metal turns into a magnet. Turn the electricity off and whatever it was holding drops. I built a small crane that works this way.

Then I changed one thing at a time, more wire, more electricity, a different metal in the middle, and wrote down what each change did. The whole thing is a webpage instead of a folder in my room.
