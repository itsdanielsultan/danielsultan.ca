Making finance easier to study.

Alongside my own finance study, I built InvestmentRegulation.ca and IFC Prep. They bring lessons, explanations and practice into one place, with a clear route through technical material.

InvestmentRegulation.ca covers the Canadian Investment Regulatory Exam. IFC Prep is a separate Investment Funds in Canada companion. Both are free, with progress saved in the learner’s browser.

Making Mississauga’s growth visible.

The same interest in clear information led me to Future Mississauga, an interactive 3D atlas of buildings, transit and development proposals.

I combine public data, development drawings and my own modelling, then make the sources and remaining estimates visible.

The visualisations were featured by 905HUB in September 2026.

Caps Lock for Raycast.

A small Mac utility that keeps Caps Lock available when its physical key is remapped. I built it with TypeScript and a native Swift helper, and contributed it to Raycast’s public extensions repository.
