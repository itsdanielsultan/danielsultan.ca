The main project · August 2026

investmentregulation.ca

69Lessons
100Questions
8Chapters
3Weeks

The CIRE is the exam you need to pass to move up in financial sales in Canada. I'm taking it, and the material you're given to study from is three PDF documents that list what you're expected to know without ever explaining any of it.

So I wrote a course that starts at "what is investing" and gets you to the exam. Along the way there are flashcards that come back more often when you get them wrong, a drill mode with no timer, a timed round, and a full mock exam you can review afterwards.

Nothing you do on it gets sent anywhere. Your progress is saved in your own browser, there's no account, and there's nothing to pay.
