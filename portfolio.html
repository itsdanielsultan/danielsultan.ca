Oct 20263D atlas · Ongoing

Future Mississauga

Live website · Independent city visualisation

An independent visual atlas of Mississauga, with 19 explorable areas, skyline comparisons and an interactive 3D view. October’s updates added reference-based building studies, Lisgar & Argentia coverage and clearer building faces in the viewer, alongside public source and review notes.

I direct the project, combining my Blender modelling with City data, project drawings and credited assets. AI assists with geometry repairs, conversion, rendering scripts and website implementation; I review and refine the results.

The atlas distinguishes existing buildings, construction and proposals. It is a way to explore possible change, not an official plan or a prediction that every proposal will be built.

Oct 2026 Study tool · Live

IFC Prep

Free, open-source Investment Funds in Canada study companion

I built on the learning approach behind InvestmentRegulation.ca with 54 lessons across 18 chapters, a separate 324-question practice bank and 162 flashcards. Worked examples, written exercises and mock papers give learners different ways to practise the same ideas.

Progress and unfinished sessions stay in the browser, with exportable backups and printable notes. The downloaded HTML works offline. It is an independent companion, not an official CSI product or a replacement for the purchased course.

Aug 2026 Website · Current

InvestmentRegulation.ca

Live · Free, open-source CIRE study website

A study resource for the Canadian Investment Regulatory Exam, starting with the basics and building through 69 plain-language lessons across eight chapters. I wanted the explanations to teach the ideas before testing them.

The site includes 100 practice questions, flashcards, short drills and a mock exam. It needs no account or payment, and progress stays in the learner's own browser.

Oct 13, 2026 Extension · Published

Caps Lock for Raycast

Available in the Raycast Store · Merged upstream

Remapping Caps Lock to another shortcut should not mean losing Caps Lock itself. This extension turns it on or off from Raycast or a hotkey, without changing the user's keyboard mappings.

A TypeScript command calls a native Swift helper, checks the resulting keyboard state and reports it. The implementation handles overlapping requests and stalled calls. Raycast merged the contribution and published it on October 13.

Jun 2026 Physical game

Synapse board-game prototype

Stage: physical prototype · Informal classmate testing

One player makes a pattern with shape tiles. The other studies it, then rebuilds the pattern from memory after the board is cleared. I designed the pieces and built the prototype by hand for under $12.

I tested grids from 2×2 to 5×5 with classmates. The smallest board was too easy; nobody completed the 5×5 board perfectly on a first attempt. Those sessions led me to replace pass-or-fail scoring with an accuracy percentage.

Jun 2026 Physics project

Electromagnetic physics demonstration

Stage: completed school project · Working crane, experiment and project site

I built a small crane that lifts metal when current runs through a coil and releases it when the power is switched off.

I changed one variable at a time, including the amount of wire, the current and the core material, recorded what changed, and published the hypothesis, procedure, results and prototype photographs as a website.

Dec 2025 Shortcut templates

Pieoneer shortcut collection

Published open-source collection · Eight Mac apps

Pieoneer opens a wheel of commands around the cursor when a key is held. I made and published a collection of shortcut wheels for the apps I use most, so common actions stay close to the pointer.

The editable templates cover Beeper, ChatGPT Atlas, Craft, Dia, Finder, Notes, Safari and Sketch. This is a shortcut collection for Pieoneer, not the Pieoneer application itself.
