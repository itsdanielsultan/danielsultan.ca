InvestmentRegulation.ca

Stage: live · Free, open-source CIRE study website

The official exam material says what people need to know, but does not teach it from the beginning. I turned the syllabus into 69 plain-language lessons across eight chapters.

The finished site includes 100 practice questions, flashcards, short drills and a mock exam. It needs no account or payment, and progress stays in the learner's own browser.

Jun 2026 Physical game

Synapse board-game prototype

Stage: physical prototype · Informal classmate testing

One player makes a pattern with shape tiles. The other studies it, then rebuilds the pattern from memory after the board is cleared. I designed the pieces and built the prototype by hand for under $12.

I tested grids from 2×2 to 5×5 with classmates. The smallest board was too easy; nobody completed the 5×5 board perfectly on a first attempt. Those sessions led me to replace pass-or-fail scoring with an accuracy percentage.

Jun 2026 Physics project

Electromagnetic physics demonstration

Stage: completed school project · Working crane, experiment and project site

I built a small crane that lifts metal when current runs through a coil and releases it when the power is switched off.

I changed one variable at a time, including the amount of wire, the current and the core material, recorded what changed, and published the hypothesis, procedure, results and prototype photographs as a website.
