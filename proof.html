A city model you can inspect

Future Mississauga · Ongoing
OngoingInteractive atlas

Sources, revisions and open questions

The live atlas combines an interactive explorer, neighbourhood views and existing-and-proposed comparisons. A public review records sources and remaining approximations; dated studies show how individual models changed.

The current atlas includes Churchill Meadows, Lisgar & Argentia, Meadowvale Village and East Credit & Creditview. Reference-based studies show how Winding Trail and other individual buildings changed as better information became available.

Published in Raycast

October 13, 2026
ReleasedCaps Lock

A utility in the official extension Store

Caps Lock toggles the Mac’s native Caps Lock state even when the physical key has been remapped. The Raycast Store lists me as its author, and the original contribution was merged into Raycast’s public extension repository.

The command pairs TypeScript with a native Swift helper. It checks the resulting state and handles failed or overlapping invocations.

Jun 2026Informal trials

Finding the right difficulty

I tried grids from 2×2 to 5×5 with classmates. The smallest worked as a tutorial; the largest was difficult to reproduce perfectly. That led to accuracy-based scoring, so remembering most of a pattern still counts.

The build article records these informal trials and the changes they led to.
