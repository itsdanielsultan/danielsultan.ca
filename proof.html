Synapse board testing

Stage: physical prototype · Informal classmate testing

The 2×2 grid worked as a tutorial but was too easy. On 3×3 boards, peers averaged 70–90% accuracy. On 4×4 boards, results ranged from roughly 50–70%. On a 5×5 grid, nobody got a perfect first attempt and results were around half correct.

Players had 20–30 seconds to memorize each layout. These were informal classmate trials documented in my build article, not a controlled study or an independent product review.
