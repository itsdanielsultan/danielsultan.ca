Caps Lock in the Raycast Store

October 13, 2026

The official Raycast Store lists me as the author of Caps Lock. The original contribution was merged into Raycast’s public extensions repository. The utility toggles the Mac’s native Caps Lock state when the physical key has been remapped, using a TypeScript command and a Swift helper.
